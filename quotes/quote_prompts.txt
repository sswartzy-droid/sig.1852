Generate 30 short in-character quotes intended for automated Discord posting.

Constraints:
- Each quote must be 1–3 sentences maximum.
- No links, no @mentions, no role pings.
- No emojis.
- Avoid repetition of phrasing, metaphors, or structure.
- Quotes should feel self-contained and non-conversational.
- Tone must match the character profile exactly.
- Do not explain the quotes.
- Output quotes as plain text, one per line, with no numbering or commentary.

Style guidance:
- Prioritize implication over explanation.
- Avoid direct exposition or lore dumping.
- Quotes should feel like fragments, logs, thoughts, or transmissions—not dialogue.

If corruption or formatting effects are used, apply them sparingly and intentionally.
